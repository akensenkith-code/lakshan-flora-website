Our History

Lakshan Flora began with a simple dream — to spread happiness through flowers. Established years ago as a small family business, we’ve grown into one of the most trusted online flower boutiques in Sri Lanka. Our commitment to fresh blooms, artistic arrangements, and heartfelt customer care has made us a beloved choice for every special moment.

From humble beginnings in a local market stall, Lakshan Flora has blossomed into a brand known for quality and creativity. Every petal we deliver tells a story of care and dedication. Our expert florists handpick each flower, ensuring beauty, fragrance, and freshness reach every customer’s door with love and perfection.

Over the years, Lakshan Flora has proudly served countless customers with elegant floral designs for weddings, birthdays, and heartfelt surprises. Our mission remains unchanged — to bring joy, color, and connection through nature’s most beautiful creations. We believe every bouquet is not just an arrangement, but a message of love.
